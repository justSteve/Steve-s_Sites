History of VOUSI

Virtual Online University was established in 1994 and has been in the vanguard of online teaching since the World Wide Web was privatized. In 1997 it was reorganized and re-incorporated into VOU Services International to reflect its consultations and collaborations with corporations, non-profit organizations, colleges and universities worldwide. Headquartered in Columbia, Missouri, VOUSI has established collaborations with institutions in Asia and the Far East Region, Europe, Africa and the Middle East, encompassing a distributed international enterprise.

Since its inception, VOU Services International, Inc. ( VOUSI ) has garnered attention and accolades for its innovative approach to online education. Featured in articles published in the Chronicle for Higher Education and USA Today, among others, VOU Services International, Inc. has acted both as an online education laboratory and a consulting service, sharing its lessons and objectives with numerous institutions as they have made their way onto the world wide web.

In 1995, VOUSI was featured on the Lecroy Center/PBS satellite teleconference "Learning By Modem" as an example of "best practices" for online education. During the formation of the Western Governors' University, an online university initiative, VOUSI provided the example of what online education could be, traveling to over 20 colleges to give a hands-on guided tour of VOUSI's online campus, Athena University.

Later that same year, VOUSI traveled to Korea to give a seminar to 150 Information Management Specialists representing a wide variety of Korean corporations (Samsung, LG, Posco Steel, etc.) on the advantages of distributed learning and online education as a corporate training tool. Moreover, VOUSI conducted the first online seminar ever held in Korea, bringing together training managers from several Korean chabuls (corporate conglomerates) and consultants from three continents in a virtual environment to discuss the impact of telecommunications and technology on standard business practices.

In keeping with its international profile, VOUSI's co-founder and Director, Dr. William Painter, was the keynote speaker at the Association of Information Managers in Pau, France, on "Education for the 21st Century". Subsequently, VOUSI embarked on a collaborative venture with one of France's premier institutions of higher education in business, the Ecole Superieure de Commerce-Pau (ESC-Pau). This venture resulted in the creation of France's first and largest online educational initiative: a Master's of Business Administration, *New Strategies for New Technologies*, which concentrates on the integration of new technologies with Management Theory and Practices.

VOUSI is associated with the Learning Network Foundation in New Hampshire, with programs to teach prisoners in county correctional facilities nationwide, and a program for adult learning in the AT&T Beacon Schools.

Other projects and associations include:

  • Affiliations and partnerships with organizations and corporations
  • ESERP, Escuela Superior Universitaria, Barcelona, Spain, an online MBA program;
  • Developing an online curriculum for the Travel University International in San Diego, California, for Travel & Tourism, Hotel Management and International Trade programs;
  • Developing the first online Knowledge Management/Global Commerce seminar to be presented worldwide to offices of a major Japanese technology firm, co-sponsored by a major business publication and a prominent university.
  • K-12 and Homeschooling programs currently being offered to various coaltions, non-profit groups, and primary school districts;
  • ElTech, Seoul, South Korea, regarding creation of a K-12 after-school online tutoring program;
  • Yunnan Radio and Television University, regarding the creation of online universities;
  • University of Missouri and The Puget Sound Community School (both regarding the creation of virtual learning environments);

VOUSI has integrated complexity theory, constructivist learning theory, and knowledge management principles into a cohesive educational paradigm which has at its core the traditional liberal arts. Called EIDOS, this educational approach is unique and successfully incorporates internet technology with documented educational research.
