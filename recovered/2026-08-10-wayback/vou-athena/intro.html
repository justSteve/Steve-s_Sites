Virtual Online University Services International, Inc. (VOUSI) is a Virtual Corporation operating as a Corporate University. VOUSI encompasses three distinct entities:
  • VOUSI: the corporation - VOUSI offers training and consulting services for corporations and non-profit agencies globewide, with an emphasis on distributed complex systems management. VOUSI also sponsors Athena University and Athena Academy ...
  • Athena University - AU is a non-profit institution offering undergraduate and graduate programs in a fully online, fully interactive environment ...
  • Athena Academy - AA offers K-12 and Homeschooling services through a Student-centered, constructivist Learning Community ...

VOUSI offers a novel and effective approach to academic excellence, professional development and life-long learning. Conventional distance education programs leave one isolated, and few are based on *interactive* real-time instruction online. VOUSI uses an enhanced Virtual Education Environment (VEE) as our electronic campus. A VEE allows one-on-one collaboration, debate, and interaction between fellow students and instructors by way of a distinctly innovative model for distance education.

VOUSI has a number of Strategic Alliances through its Friends and Partners. Through our Friends and Partners, we are helping to build a globally distributed system of interactive education, training and technology programs, encompassing organisational planning, extranets and intranets, information systems -- including distributed systems, workflow management systems and enterprise modelling methods -- and best practices.

VOUSI offers consulting and development services in:

  • Corporate Training
  • Corporate University Design and Implementation
  • Knowledge Management and Distributed Systems Design
  • Global Business Communications

A Distributed Corporation

VOUSI is a distributed corporation which maintains virtual offices, conducts meetings online, and engages in educational activities appropriate to an information economy. We at VOUSI recognize that the greatest asset of any organization is an adaptive and educated staff. Management is required which is capable of effective and far-sighted decision making and people who can readily adapt to the rapidly changing demands of a global economy.

Founded on over two millennia of educational practices and traditions, VOUSI's philosophy eschews faddish trends which primarily constitute modern corporate 'training.' Instead, VOUSI is dedicated to integrating the educational experience with the pragmatics of the workplace. We think that it is more effective to have a work force capable of being redirected towards new tasks, than to terminate and rehire. Moreover, managers face increasingly difficult decisions, having to calculate not only the impact of their actions on the fiscal health of the organization, but also the long-range implications for culture and economy on the global scale. Such higher-ordered processes may never be achieved through simple 'training' seminars which, though temporarily satisfying, fail to provide managers the tools with which to meet unfamiliar challenges.
