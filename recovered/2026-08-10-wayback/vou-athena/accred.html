Accreditation

VOUSI offers an accredited MBA program conferred by the Ecole Superieure de Commerce-Pau. The program is recognized and accredited by the Ministry of Education (France), the Conference de Grande Ecoles, and the European Federation for Management Development (AACSB's European counterpart).

VOUSI will seek accreditation for its other programs when regional accrediting agencies become more familiar with online education such that they can intelligently evaluate both content and delivery beyond the 'correspondence course' context.
