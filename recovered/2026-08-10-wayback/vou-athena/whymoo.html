Why use a MOO for education?

In the previous scenarios, a few concepts become clear. First of all, the isolation of the distance education student is removed as students and teachers become a part of a virtual community. They do not receive packets in the mail from an impersonal instructor nor do they sit and watch a television screen while a teacher lectures. Rather, they are active, participating members.

Secondly, students and teachers alike are able to integrate the environment with instructional materials such that learning and living become a seamless cloth. Instructional materials may be accessed from home, work or from anywhere that the student can use a computer. The Internet has been compared to a global library, and it is within this library that students learn and interact, pulling into the seminar materials which exist at their very fingertips.

Finally, the limitations placed on geographical location are removed and students can receive instruction from highly qualified educators regardless of their physical location.

Above all, we recognize the importance of making and strengthening connections. Whether between individuals and groups, between people and the technology they use, or between people and ideas, we've created a space that facilitates those connections by literally building a web that weaves all those components together.

Students who participate in our dynamic environment emphasizing peer collaboration and active learning not only learn the subject matter more thoroughly, they are better prepared to play a productive role it today's team-oriented, inter-dependent and inter-connected environment.
