Athena University is administered by VOU Services International, Inc.. Athena shares VOUSI's mission to provide a quality liberal arts education to students in a more cost-effective and accessible manner. To accomplish this mission, Athena has established a virtual education environment (VEE) which, thanks to present telecommunications technology, can be accessed from anywhere on the globe at any time.

Founded in the Spring of 1995, Athena is a young organization and is presently applying for accreditation. Articles about us have appeared in the Chronicle of Higher Education, USA Today, and Investors Weekly (among others), describing the exciting educational opportunities Athena offers.

For an in depth look at how classes are conducted at Athena University consult the Road Map to On-Line Learning.

   Course Work at AU
  The faculty of Athena University believe strongly in the value of a liberal arts education. Thus courses emphasize the development of critical thinking skills and programs of study integrate vocational and professional course work with the exploration of literature, math, science and the arts. Courses are offered in 13 week terms and short-terms of varying lengths and are conducted on-line at our VEE.

   Faculty
  The faculty at Athena University are dedicated scholars who believe that the primary focus of a University is its students. Naturally, research and service to the community are also important interests. If you are a forward thinking, innovative educator, interested in close contact with your students in an exciting new medium and are interested in Athena University as a prospective faculty member, please contact VOUSI.
