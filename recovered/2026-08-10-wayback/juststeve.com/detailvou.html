In 1994 I joined an unfunded startup - Virtual Online University - as Director of Communication with responsibilities in strategic positioning, marketing, and online training/orientation processes. It was exciting work and we quickly gathered a core of about a dozen individuals contributing significant amounts of sweat equity.

We gained the attention of various national and international clients and gave a series of technology demonstrations to institutions affiliated with The Western Governor's University and numerous demos to other renowned educational institutions. We designed and presented an Online Seminar covering Net-based training & educational issues for a South Korean company consulting for Samsung.

My internet design and writing skills figured prominently throughout this period. I excelled at site promotion, design and meta-tagging techniques that gained us high visibility. I designed compelling, effective tutorials and instructional procedures. Internally, I gained a reputation for an ability to overcome the obstacles of the medium and deliver -on time- innovative solutions.
