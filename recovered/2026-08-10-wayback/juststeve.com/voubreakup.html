Below is the message I sent during the dissoultion of the founding Virtual Online University team in July of 1996...a painful incident that grew out of a struggle between the rightful Board of Directors and Bill Painter, the Executive Director. It's a soap opera....read on at you're own risk.

To summarize a very convoluted situation: A client of VOU's -Kwan Lee of South Korean business ElTech- made serious allegations of financial misappropriations on the part of Mr. Painter. Many within VOU's staff and administration where convinced of the veracity of Mr. Lee's claims. When Mr. Painter was challanged he responded with unilateral moves to fire or otherwise remove from authority his accusers. He directed his staff technician to forge an email to InterNIC to have the domain name registration changed so as to maintain control of the internet presence.

He sued the board who failed to raise funds to fight the suit and, in that sense, Mr. Painter "won".

First let me say that my goal is not to present proof of acts that would hold up in a court of law. Instead I'll try to establish that Bill has committed actions that disqualify him as a viable leader of Athena/VOU. I'm still hopeful the board will choose to remove Bill from any association with the institution so that I can continue to devote my efforts here.

Many of the accusations of misconduct surround Bill's transactions with Dr. Kwan Lee who, up until about 2 mouths ago, was our VP of Pacific Rim affairs. Since Bill's 1st trip to Korea he has been working to create a project referred to as Children's Kingdom. Kwan says he gave Bill $5,000 to start the project. Bill has consistently said he'd gotten no money from Kwan except for travel and lodging expenses.

Perhaps best just to link in the message from Kwan that details his treatment at Bill's hands.

Why would Bill risk so much to skim money? In part, probably because Kwan paid him cash and Bill never expected to have to account for it. Why would Kwan make such payments in cash? It's a common Korean business practice for tax purposes.

If it comes down to one man's word against another's, Bill has told enough untruths to enough different people, that the decision, for me, is easy.

For example, Bill has been telling me (and others) for 3-4 months that the French project was to be a $200,000 pilot project and that once we got the those funds (approval is just a formality, I was assured) all of us at the administrative level would begin to draw a salary. The fact is that the pilot project was always designed to be just $20,000 and obviously that won't go very far. Had I known the true scope of the project I would have realized that I had to seek paying employment elsewhere until VOU really got off the ground. Instead, Bill used fraudulent promises to retain my uncompensated services up to this point.

What Bill has shown (and _not in isolated incidents) is either the willingness to blatantly mis-represent the facts or the inability to discern them.

Here are other facts that strike me as important:

Last summer Bill told Anne she would get a specific percentage of the revenues of the conference we hosted. Afterwards, when attendance was not as great as was hoped (but still there was _some_ revenue) he just told her there wasn't enough and paid her nothing.

Anne taught a student who paid for a class. Bill didn't pay Anne.

Bill's Children's Kingdom project which Kwan gave him cash to work on was either a VOU project or it wasn't. If it was, Bill embezzled the money. If it wasn't he used his position as an officer of VOU to compete directly against VOU. Recall that Bill fired Tina because she used (he says) VOU's name inappropriately.

Bill told us that all revenues from the Korean seminar stayed with Kwan to cover publicity expenses. We know from Kwan's accounting (Bill didn't challenge that aspect of Kwan's message to him) that Bill got $2,200. No one else got a cent to even cover expenses incurred.

But consider an even more fundamental aspect of Bill's performance: Bill's been at this for 2 years now. He has had some minor success in attracting attention to the institution but the only tangible thing is a 20K pilot project leading to a disputable wider project.

As a leader internally he's either fired outright (Tina and Anne - both on questionable grounds), freeze out (Ray, Gerson, Swatez who should, as Business Dean, be officially involved in the French project) or disaffected them so completely that they've left (Rana, Duckett).

Look at the quality of the folks left around him. Well meaning as they may be, they are clearly not up to the task of dealing with the concrete problems facing us.

Bill's actions since he began to feel the pressure that has been the natural result of his deceptions have clearly indicated a confrontational response. Donnelly's sudden return as "President". Yanking Ray's access to the server that runs the MOO in the name of (sic) security. And the latest, most blatant misdeed is Spiff forging Dan Gerson's name in an attempt to gain control of the Athena.edu domain name.

So either the board is going to dismiss him, which means there will be work to do reorganizing and picking up the pieces, or we have to talk about the possibility of starting from (almost) scratch as a separate institution.

I've come to have the greatest respect for Jerry's grasp of the opportunities I'm still convinced face an on-line organization. I also have the utmost respect for the people I've worked closely with in the past year. Regardless of the official VOU/Athena outcome I'm as excited by the possibilities facing us as I am sick and tired of talking and writing about the mess we are wallowing in right now.
