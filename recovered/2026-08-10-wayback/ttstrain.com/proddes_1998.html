The first comprehensive CD-ROM training series designed exclusively for the financial marketplace. For free previews/evaluations of any of these titles, please call (800)-831-0678 or fill out our on-line form.
The Color of Money
Teaches the basics of correctly identifying different denominations, how to spot counterfeit money, and what to do when the employee encounters a suspected counterfeiter. Every person on a bank's staff will benefit from this selection. This program includes the new currency issued by the Treasury.
Endorsements
Covers the correct and incorrect types of check endorsements, with strong emphasis upon the countless ways customers sign checks. See how a little information can greatly protect your financial institution against fraudulent or improper endorsements.
Common Balancing Errors
This program helps tellers overcome one of their most frustrating tasks-- balancing their cash drawer. This instructional module details the basics of balancing, describes some typical balancing efforts, explores some common reasons for balancing mistakes, and shows how to safeguard against balancing errors.
Robbery
What should employees know about the unthinkable? How should they prepare? What procedures should they follow should a robbery actually occur? This program covers all the vital steps - a must program for training staff under the requirements of the Bank Protection Act.
Telephone Skills
Answering the phone is a task done hundreds of times a day at every bank. This comprehensive training module instructs employees on how to use the phone correctly in a variety of situations.
Opening New Accounts
This training module will equip your staff with the knowledge to confidently open new accounts which may help protect banks from avoidable legal action. Training information includes such key issues as account ownership, account options and legal documentation.
