Mark Bennett - President
As the founder of MemberServ, Mark Bennett has been involved in bank training for over 18 years. Mark has a BBA degree from the University of Wisconsin - Whitewater. He began his training career with a bank holding company in 1979. His responsibilities included teller and lender training. After leaving the bank, Mark was employed by Financial Education & Development. He wrote and conducted training seminars throughout the country for over four years. In 1987, he developed the company's first video training tape and has now been involved in the production of eighteen different videos. MemberServ was started in 1987 and was acquired by Demco, Inc. in 1989. Mark began researching the use of multimedia for training in 1994. By 1995, MemberServ had developed it's first CD-ROM training product. In April of 1997, MemberServ became an independent company, Total Training Solutions L.L.C.

Rich Trace - Product Manager
Rich Trace joined MemberServ in January 1994 and is responsible for overseeing project management. Rich has a BBA degree from the University of Wisconsin in marketing and management administration. He has managed the production of many video and multimedia projects. Some of his responsibilities include project planning, scheduling, team coordination and budgeting.

Stephen Scripp - Multimedia Consultant
Steve serves as the initial contact between the client and Total Training Solutions. As part of the TTS team for over 1 1/2 years, Steve makes sure every client receives the personal attention they deserve. Steve has a B.A. degree from the University of Wisconsin.

Scott Smith - Multimedia Developer
Product authoring is the responsibility of Scott Smith. Scott's skills include various authoring tools and programming languages. Scott has a degree from the University of Wisconsin - Oshkosh.
