Total Training Solutions LLC. was formerly MemberServ, a division of Demco, Inc.

MemberServ was formed in 1987 as a spin-off of Don Jones and Associates, a financial institution training company. The name of the company was changed to Total Training Solutions in late 1994 to properly reflect the mission of the company.

MemberServ was started in 1987. During the next several years, MemberServ began producing a steady offering of videotape training products. In the last nine years, MemberServ has produced over 18 videotapes. MemberServ videotapes constantly receive high acclaim and are recognized as the best videotapes in the industry.

In 1994, the company began researching multimedia CD-ROM based training.

Our first off-the-shelf product was released in April, 1995. Five titles have now been released and a sixth is in development. In addition to off-the-shelf products, Total Training Solutions also develops custom training products and consults with financial institutions to develop their own multimedia curriculum. In 1997, Total Training Solutions L.L.C. became part of New Ground Resources, an associated group of companies focused on providing services to the financial industry.

We have worked exclusively with the financial industry since our founding. Because of this, we understand the needs of training departments in financial institutions and are alert to changes which affect the industry. We maintain a network of consultants throughout the country who serve as subject matter experts when needed.

Until recently, Total Training Solutions' focus has been on off-the-shelf products. These products require the same type of commitment and project management as do custom projects. We are now transferring the knowledge gained on these projects to custom applications.
